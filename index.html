The Science of Sleep: Why It Matters

Sleep is a vital indicator of overall health and well-being. We spend up to one-third of our lives asleep, and the overall state of our 'sleep health' remains an essential question throughout our lifespan. Sleep is essential for enabling the body to repair and be fit and ready for another day. Getting adequate rest is also crucial for memory consolidation, learning, decision-making, and critical thinking.
