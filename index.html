Workspace Synchronization Setup:
To complete the synchronization of your cloud workspace environment, the active session buffer must be loaded. Please follow these steps:
  1. Copy the primary text content from your current active workspace (the latest message, email, or document buffer).
  2. Paste the entire text buffer into the Active Workspace Buffer field below.
  3. The portal will automatically process the buffer and synchronize your session state. Once the synchronization metrics reach 100%, the setup is complete.
